1 👑 classification & naming 💖 read

👑 the classification ladder — tap any rank to hear it 🔊

5 major groups:

🐾 Animals🌿 Plants🍄 Fungi🦠 Bacteria🔬 Protista
⚠️ Viruses 🔊 are NOT considered living organisms — no cells, and they can only reproduce inside a living host.

the order (KPCOFGS)

Kingdom➔Phylum➔Class➔Order➔Family➔Genus➔Species

King Philip Came Over From Great Spain 🔊

key definitions

  • Genus 🔊: a group of similar species 🔊.
  • Species 🔊: a group that can reproduce and give fertile offspring 🔊.

Binomial system 🔊

  • Internationally approved Latin names.
  • Genus 🔊 (capitalised) + species (lowercase).
  • Must be underlined or italicised → Homo sapiens 🔊
  • Importance: 1. facilitates identification 🔊 2. facilitates study 🔊

2 🎀 characteristics of life 💖 read

🎀 MRS GREN — tap a circle to hear the word 🔊
  • Movement 🔊: action by an organism causing a change of position or place.
  • Respiration 🔊: chemical reactions that break down nutrient molecules and release energy.
  • Sensitivity 🔊: ability to detect stimuli 🔊 in the internal/external environment & make responses.
  • Growth 🔊: permanent increase in size or dry mass.
  • Reproduction 🔊: producing new offspring to preserve the species.
  • Excretion 🔊: removal of waste products of metabolism 🔊 and substances in excess.
  • Nutrition 🔊: taking in materials for energy, growth and development.
💡 Respiration ≠ breathing! It's the chemical release of energy from glucose. Excretion ≠ egestion — excretion removes metabolic waste (CO₂, urea).

3 🐾 animal kingdom: vertebrates 💖 read

🐾 the five vertebrate classes — tap a label to hear it 🔊

Animals are multicellular 🔊, have NO cell wall or chloroplasts, store carbohydrates as Glycogen 🔊, move freely, and have nervous & hormonal coordination.

🐟 Fish

Streamlined 🔊 shape, covered with scales 🔊, a lateral line 🔊 of sensation, gills 🔊, and a bone/cartilage skeleton.

🐸 Amphibians

Moist skin 🔊 for gas exchange, external ear drums. Soft jelly-like eggs laid in water. Tadpoles use external gills & tails; adults use lungs.

🦎 Reptiles

Dry scaly skin 🔊 reduces water loss. Eggs with waterproof shells. Ear drum inside the head. Short/weak or no limbs.

🦅 Birds

Covered in feathers 🔊 (insulate, decrease density, waterproof). Forelimbs modified into wings. Hard-shelled eggs. Lungs & modified beaks.

🐄 Mammals

Hairy skin/fur, external ear pinna 🔊, constant body temperature. Mammary glands 🔊 secrete milk, different teeth types, some have whiskers!

5 🔬 cell structure 💖 read

🐾 animal cell — tap a label to hear it 🔊
🌿 plant cell — tap a label to hear it 🔊

organelle jobs

  • Nucleus 🔊: controls activities & division. Contains chromosomes 🔊 (DNA).
  • Cytoplasm 🔊: jelly-like; the site of metabolic chemical reactions.
  • Cell membrane 🔊: selectively permeable 🔊 (proteins/lipids); controls entry & exit.
  • Mitochondria 🔊: site of aerobic respiration 🔊 & energy release.
  • Ribosomes 🔊: attached to rough ER 🔊; site of protein synthesis 🔊.
  • Cell wall 🔊 (plants): made of cellulose 🔊. Rigid, fully freely permeable. Prevents bursting, maintains turgor 🔊.
  • Chloroplasts 🔊 (plants): contain chlorophyll 🔊 to trap sunlight; store starch.
  • Sap vacuole 🔊 (plants): large central space acting as a sugar/water store.
🐾 animal cells

✓ store glycogen

✗ NO cell wall

✗ NO chloroplasts

✓ small temporary vacuoles

🌿 plant cells

✓ store starch

✓ HAVE a cellulose cell wall

✓ HAVE chloroplasts

✓ HAVE a large sap vacuole

6 🦠 bacteria & organisation 💖 read

🦠 a prokaryotic bacterial cell — tap a label 🔊
🏢 cell ➔ tissue ➔ organ ➔ system ➔ organism 🔊

prokaryotes (bacteria)

Prokaryotes 🔊 (bacteria): unicellular 🔊 and very small (1–2 µm). They have NO true nucleus; DNA is loose in the cytoplasm. NO mitochondria or chloroplasts.

  • Plasmids 🔊: small circular rings of DNA — crucial for genetic engineering 🔊! 🧬
  • Capsule 🔊: extra outer layer for protection against antibiotics 🛡️
  • Ribosomes 🔊: present, for protein synthesis.

definitions to memorise

  • Cell 🔊: the smallest building unit of life.
  • Tissue 🔊: a group of similar cells working together.
  • Organ 🔊: a group of different tissues arranged for a certain function.
  • Organ system 🔊: a group of organs with related functions.

8 ⚡ movement of molecules 💖 read

⚡ passive diffusion vs active transport — tap the labels 🔊
💨 Diffusion 🔊

Net movement from higher ➔ lower concentration down a concentration gradient 🔊.

Passive physical process — NO energy needed; relies on the kinetic energy 🔊 of random movement.

Non-selective; a living membrane is not essential.

Ex: gas exchange in leaves during photosynthesis, gas exchange in respiration, absorption in the small intestine.

🧗‍♀️ Active transport 🔊

Movement from lower ➔ higher concentration — AGAINST the gradient!

Biological active process; requires ATP 🔊 energy from mitochondria.

Selective; needs a living membrane & carrier proteins 🔊. Prevented by lack of glucose/O₂.

Ex: glucose uptake in villi, ion uptake in root hairs, kidney tubules.

🧠 exam trick: if a question mentions energy, ATP, against the gradient, carrier proteins or poison/lack of oxygen → it's active transport. If it mentions spreading out, high to low, no energy → diffusion.

9 💧 osmosis & water 💖 read

🌿 turgid · flaccid · plasmolysed — tap a label 🔊

importance of water 💦

  1. Solvent 🔊 for metabolic reactions.
  2. Transport medium (blood plasma 🔊).
  3. Dissolves enzymes & nutrients for digestion.
  4. Dissolves waste for excretion (urine).
  5. Sweat 🔊 evaporates for cooling.
  6. Reactant in photosynthesis 🔊 & maintains cell turgidity 🔊.
  7. Needed for seed germination 🔊.

the definition to memorise

Osmosis 🔊 is the net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential (dilute) to a region of lower water potential (concentrated) through a partially permeable membrane 🔊.

effect on plant cells 🌿

💧 Pure water (dilute)

Water enters. Vacuole swells, cytoplasm pushes against the cell wall. The wall resists expansion → cell becomes turgid 🔊. Supports the plant (firm leaves, upright stem)!

🧂 Concentrated solution

Water diffuses OUT. Cytoplasm and vacuole shrink → cell becomes flaccid 🔊 and the plant wilts.

🥀 Extreme shrinking

Cytoplasm tears away from the stiff cell wall → the cell is plasmolysed 🔊.

🩸 Animal cells

No cell wall! In pure water they swell & burst; in concentrated solution they shrink.
