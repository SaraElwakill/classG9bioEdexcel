1 🧁 Biological Molecules

🧪 What are your snacks made of? — tap to hear 🔊
🍞 Carbohydrates🥩 Proteins🧈 Lipids🍊 Vitamins
  • Carbohydrates 🔊 (C, H, O): Ratio of H to O is 2:1.
    • Monosaccharides (Glucose) & Disaccharides (Sucrose, Maltose) are sweet & soluble.
    • Polysaccharides (Starch, Glycogen, Cellulose) are not sweet & insoluble.
    • Energy supply: 1g = 17kJ 🔊.
  • Proteins 🔊 (C, H, O, N): Built from Amino Acids 🔊 (20 types). Used for growth, tissue repair, enzymes, and antibodies.
  • Lipids / Fats 🔊 (C, H, O): 1 Glycerol + 3 Fatty Acids.
    • Unsaturated fats precipitate less blood cholesterol than saturated fats. Energy: 1g = 39kJ.

🧪 Food Tests Lab

🍬 Glucose: Add Benedict's 🔊 + Boiling water bath. Turns Brick Red.
🥔 Starch: Add Iodine 🔊. Turns Blue-Black.
🥩 Protein: Add Biuret 🔊. Turns Purple.
🧈 Lipids: Ethanol Emulsion 🔊. Turns into a Cloudy white emulsion.
🍋 Vitamin C: Add DCPIP 🔊. Turns from Blue to Colourless.

2 🧚‍♀ Enzyme Magic

🧚‍♀️ The Lock and Key model — tap to hear 🔊
⚡ Biological Catalyst🔑 Active Site🌡 Optimum🔥 Denatured
  • Enzymes 🔊 are proteins that act as Biological Catalysts 🔊. They speed up reactions without being changed!
  • Lock & Key: The substrate 🔊 fits exactly into the enzyme's Active Site 🔊.
🌡️ Temperature: As heat rises to the Optimum (37°C in humans), molecules gain kinetic energy and collide more. Above optimum, the active site loses its shape. The enzyme is Denatured 🔊 and stops working!
🧪 pH Level: Highly acidic or alkaline extremes will also denature enzymes. (Pepsin prefers acidic, Trypsin prefers alkaline).
